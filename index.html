Everything your controller needs.

Practical tools, from your first connection to your next game.

◎

Live input monitor

Watch sticks, buttons, and triggers respond in real time. Select controls directly from the mapping dock.

⇄

Mapping and profiles

Remap buttons, learn a controller layout, and save local profiles or per-game mappings.

〰

Feel the rumble

Test both motors, tune their strength, and pass game vibration through to supported controllers.

⊕

Calibrate and revive

Adjust deadzones, inspect controller health, and use stick rescue and encoder testing tools.

♧

Two players, more ways to play

Use Xbox 360 or DualShock 4 virtual output with two-player support and shared arcade encoders.

▣

A clear Windows interface

Native text and controls, a detailed controller preview, and a layout that scales to 4K displays.

Why it was created.

“No other software I tried made my stick rumble. PadRevive is the only one that did.”

That experience became the reason to build PadRevive: bring the feedback back and give budget controllers the mapping, calibration, and testing tools they need.
